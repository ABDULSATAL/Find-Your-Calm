Your simple wellness guide

Small habits, meaningful changes.

Explore these lessons at your own pace. Choose what feels manageable for you.

01

Understand your stress

Stress can affect your thoughts, body, sleep, and concentration. Notice what is troubling you without judging yourself.

Try this: Write down one thing on your mind and one small action within your control.

02

Give your mind a pause

You don't have to respond to every thought immediately. A quiet moment can help you slow down and refocus.

Try this: Put away your phone for two minutes and gently notice your surroundings.

03

Build a healthier sleep routine

Try to keep regular sleeping and waking times. Make your room comfortable and reduce caffeine late in the day.

Try this: Choose a simple, relaxing activity to do before bed.

04

Look after your body

Regular meals, water, daylight, and movement can support general well-being. Adapt activities to your needs and abilities.

Try this: Stretch gently or take a short walk if you feel able.

05

Make your day manageable

A long task list can feel overwhelming. You can break a task into smaller steps and take breaks when needed.

Try this: Pick one important task to focus on today.

06

Stay connected

Talking with someone you trust may help you feel supported. It's okay to ask for help or say no when you need to.

Try this: Check in with a friend, relative, or another trusted person.

07

Keep going gently

Missing a day doesn't mean you have failed. Start again whenever you are ready and celebrate realistic progress.

Try this: Choose one habit from this guide to continue next week.

When to seek extra support

If stress, anxiety, low mood, or sleep difficulties persist, worsen, or interfere with daily life, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional.

A simple grounding exercise

Gently notice the world around you:

  • Notice five things you can see.
  • Notice four things you can feel.
  • Notice three things you can hear.
  • Notice two things you can smell.
  • Notice one thing you appreciate right now.

You can adapt this exercise to whatever feels comfortable.
